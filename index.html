      What’s actually in those codes?

      X’s new copied-link codes

      When you tap “copy link” on X, it now adds codes after the ? in the URL:

      s= says how it was shared — s=46 is the iPhone app, s=20 is desktop. t= is unique to that session and device, and is an encoded user ID. si= is also tied to the sharer.

      Only X can decode them back to a person — a stranger seeing the link just sees a jumble of letters, and there’s no phone number in it. Still, deleting everything from the ? onwards — or just the codes this page removes — leaves a link that opens the same post, without the trail.

      Stripped from most links

      • utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign — ad & campaign tracking
      • fbclid — Facebook click ID
      • gclid, gbraid, wbraid — Google Ads click IDs
      • msclkid — Microsoft Ads click ID
      • igshid — Instagram share ID
      • mc_cid, mc_eid — Mailchimp tracking
      • twclid, ttclid, yclid — X, TikTok & Yahoo click IDs

      Kept when they matter

      • Video IDs and timestamps — YouTube’s v and t stay, so the video still opens at the right moment.
      • Search terms — q on a search page stays.
      • Page and product details — anything that changes what the page shows stays.
      • Everything else that isn’t on the tracking list is left alone, rather than guessed at.

      Will the clean link still work? Yes, for ordinary pages, posts and videos. Tracking codes only record where a click came from — they don’t change the content. If a link ever needs a code to work (a password-reset or invite link, for example), don’t clean it.
